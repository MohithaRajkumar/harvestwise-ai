THE TOMATO MATURITY GUIDE

Five stages.
One evolving story.

These categories are illustrative. Actual maturity cues vary by tomato variety, intended use and local crop guidance.

STAGE 01

Immature

Mostly green surface.

STAGE 02

Breaker

First visible colour shift.

STAGE 03

Turning

Mixed green and warm tones.

STAGE 04

Ripe

Mostly red or orange-red.

STAGE 05

Overripe

May show softening or dark spots.

Colour alone cannot confirm firmness, internal quality or the best harvest time.
